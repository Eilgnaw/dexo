What's New

- Improved linux.do web-login and Cloudflare verification-state synchronization, and aligned read-time uploads with browser topic referrers, XHR, Fetch Metadata, and Discourse request headers.
- Low-frequency read-time reporting is enabled by default for linux.do web-login accounts. Uploads are consolidated, serialized, and rate-limited; API-key and anonymous sessions do not report.
- Added “Read-time Reporting” under linux.do's Me page, including a synchronized status, switch, and detailed success, failure, HTTP-status, and error history.
- When Cloudflare requests verification, the current upload pauses and offers two choices: turn reporting off or open site verification.
