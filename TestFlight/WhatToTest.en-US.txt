What to Test

- In standard post mode, scroll down and confirm the three-button bottom bar contracts with momentum into a centered Scroll to Top button. Scroll up and confirm the full bar returns smoothly.
- Tap the compact Scroll to Top button and confirm the list stops at the true content top and expands the full bar. Taking over with a drag during the return should cancel it immediately.
- On iOS 26, check the Liquid Glass merging effect as the bottom-bar buttons approach each other. Also verify tree mode, light and dark appearances, and Reduce Motion behavior.
- Like a post or topic and confirm its image flies toward the left-side target, bursts into fragments on impact, and disappears without lingering views or flashing.
- Scroll down the home list and tap the Home tab again. Confirm the first tap returns to the top and the second tap refreshes the list.
