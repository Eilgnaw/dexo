What to Test

- Sign in to a forum account that has not enabled push notifications. Confirm the original action (such as liking or replying) continues first, then the push prompt appears when the interface is free.
- Tap Enable and confirm the blocking “Enabling push notifications…” indicator appears immediately, disappears after registration, and Push Notifications settings show the account as enabled.
- Tap No Thanks on a fresh login and confirm no subscription is created and the prompt does not reappear during that login. It should appear again after a later successful login.
- Confirm accounts with an active push subscription are not prompted again, while failed or pending subscriptions can be retried.
- In post content, confirm adjacent inline code separated by source whitespace remains visually separated, for example: 发布<code>主题</code> <code>帖子</code>.
