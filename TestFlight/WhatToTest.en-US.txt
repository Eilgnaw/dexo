Read-time Reporting

- Sign in to linux.do using web login, then open Me > Read-time Reporting and confirm the switch matches the status shown on the Me page.
- Open a topic and read normally. Confirm read time is uploaded at a low frequency and that success, failure, and error details appear in Read-time Report History.
- Confirm anonymous sessions and User API Key sessions do not upload read time.
- If an upload triggers Cloudflare verification, confirm no alert interrupts reading and a circular shield remains visible across Home, topic, and Me screens. Drag it and confirm it snaps to either screen edge.
- Tap the shield and choose Open Verification or Turn Off Reporting. Cancelling verification or relaunching the app should retain the Verification Needed state and keep reporting paused; tapping Done on the verification screen should hide the shield and resume reporting.
